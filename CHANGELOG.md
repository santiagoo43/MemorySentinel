# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-25

- Work profiles with exact watch rules, notification preferences and application/group reference budgets.
- Version- and profile-specific normal ranges with explicit learning exclusions and reset controls.
- Conditional time-to-budget estimates with coverage, trend and instance-change checks.
- Flight recordings with bounded lead-in, subsequent measurements, event markers and shared-cursor review.
- Work sessions with time-weighted summaries, observation coverage and aligned comparisons.
- Observation-only five-minute Fix follow-up and comparable past-outcome summaries.
- Scoped warning snoozes, sample freshness and menu bar session/recording controls.
- Previewable local HTML/Markdown reports with private details and notes omitted by default.
- Transactional history migration, consistent recovery snapshots and pre-migration restore controls.
- Fresh kernel identity checks for reopened applications, including stale AppKit lifecycle entries.
- Recording and follow-up deadlines exclude late samples and preserve incomplete-evidence states.
- Bounded tracking of unapproved Fix plans and transactional rollback checks for failed experience writes.

## 1.1.4 — 2026-11-24

First public source and binary release.

- Native dashboard, menu bar monitor, process details and incident history.
- Manual Fix assessment, explicit actions and three-sample verification, with separate action, target and system outcomes.
- Verified normal Quit and optional reopen; separate Force Quit confirmation and process identity protection.
- Complete monitoring-repair checks: partial process readings cannot be reported as recovered.
- Synthetic scenarios for trying the workflow without real process actions.
- Bounded local storage, JSON/CSV export and interrupted-session recovery without replay.
- Centered shield icon, including the Dock and app switcher size correction.
- macOS build, test and packaging checks in GitHub Actions.
